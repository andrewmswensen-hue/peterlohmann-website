About Me

About Peter Lohmann

I bring a systems-first mindset to an industry full of operational chaos, and I help other operators do the same.

I'm the CEO of RL Property Management (750+ units), owner of Peter Lohmann Media, and co-founder of Crane, an exclusive online community for property management owners and operators.

With a background in engineering and leadership, I apply a systems-thinking lens to property management and share what's working through my twice-weekly newsletter, read by over 20,000 property management professionals.

I also host Peter Lohmann's Podcast (100+ episodes), where I interview other leaders on the real-world challenges of operating a business. My industry research includes the annual PM Trends Report, M&A Report, Largest PM Companies List, and other data-driven deep dives.

I live in a suburb of Columbus with my wife, two daughters, and our dog Oxley.
