Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the Largest PM Companies list, working with Peter, sponsoring the newsletter and podcast, and where to find everything.

The Largest PM Companies list

How do I get my company on the Largest Property Management Companies list?

Submit your company through the form on the Largest PM Companies page. The list ranks residential property management companies in the United States by the number of units (doors) they manage. Submissions are self-reported and verified where possible, and the list is refreshed each year.

How is the list ranked, and how often is it updated?

Companies are ranked by total units under management, from most to fewest. A new edition is published every year, and you can also browse the top companies by state on the same page.

Working with Peter

How can I work with Peter?

The most direct option is a paid one-hour consultation, which you can book on Peter's Calendly. For anything else, use the contact page.

What is RL Property Management (RLPM)?

RL Property Management is a residential property management company based in Columbus, Ohio, co-founded by Peter. Much of what Peter writes and talks about comes from running RLPM day to day.

Newsletter, podcast & sponsorship

How do I sponsor the newsletter or podcast?

Peter's newsletter and podcast reach thousands of property management company owners and operators. To discuss sponsorship, reach out through the contact page and mention what you would like to promote.

Can I be a guest on the podcast?

Possibly. Send a note through the contact page with a short description of who you are and what you would like to talk about.

Reports, products & community

Where can I find the Property Management M&A Report?

The report is published free on this site. You can read it at the M&A Report link in the menu.

What products does Peter offer?

Peter's products include a course on starting a property management maintenance division, a one-hour consulting session, and the free M&A report. You can see them all on the products page.

What is Crane?

Crane is a private community Peter runs for property management company owners and operators.
